## Plan

I will begin by manually migrating the SmallRye Stork project from MkDocs to Roq, and I will take note of all of the necessary steps so we know what needs to be done when designing a method to do this programatically. My general outline is as follows:

1. Convert the configuration and navigation settings from MkDocs to Roq
2. Convert the navigation structure to Roq and make webpages visible using front matter
3. Copy additional resources that may have unique formatting concerns (tables, images, etc.)
4. Change Roq's theming settings to match the MkDocs project.
